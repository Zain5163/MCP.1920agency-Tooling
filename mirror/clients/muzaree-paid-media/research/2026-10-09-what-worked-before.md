## The winning formula (Feb 2026, PKR 539–601 per sale)

- **Product:** Chelsea boots (brown + black), **in winter**.
- **Image:** dark luxury set (wood wall, gold lamp, Muzaree box), two or three pairs, brand name at the bottom.
- **One clear price on the image:** "Biggest Mega Sale · Every Pair Only **Rs. 4,499** · **No Extra Charges** ·
  Limited Stock · Limited Time Offer". Variant: "New Arrivals Included · SALE · Flat 30% OFF".
- **Copy (primary text), best performer:**
  > Some shoes just complete the outfit. And then there are shoes that change how people see you.
  > Muzaree Chelsea Boots are crafted from premium cow leather, designed for men who refuse to look ordinary.
  > Whether it's office, dinner, or a wedding event, these boots instantly upgrade your entire look.
  > Premium feel. Modern design. Confidence in every step. Cash on Delivery Available. Limited Stock Available.
  > Shop now before sizes sell out.
- **Other winning lines:** "A good outfit gets noticed. But the right shoes get remembered." · "Clean. Classic.
  Confident. Chelsea shoes that elevate your entire look. Slip on and stand out." · retargeting style: "Still
  thinking about it? The Muzaree Chelsea Boots you viewed might already be running low in stock."
- **Headlines:** "Confidence Starts From The Shoes" · "These Boots Are Selling Fast" · "Upgrade Your Style" ·
  "Premium Chelsea Shoes for Men".
- **CTA:** **Order Now** on every 2026 winner.
- **Video** versions of the same idea sold as cheaply as the posters (549, 607).

## What must change before reusing any of it (2026-10-09)

| Problem | Detail | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Price baked into the image is wrong | Winner says Rs. 4,499; Chelsea boots now **5,999** (brown, matt black), **6,599** (black suede) | Rebuild the poster in the same style with today's price |
| Dead landing page | The Feb winners link to `/collections/bags`, which is **empty** now | Link to the Chelsea boot product pages or a Chelsea collection |
| Live ad claims "**Up to 70% OFF**" | The biggest discount in the store is **57%** | Replace in round 2; never claim more than the store shows |
| "Flat 50% OFF" / "Mid Summer Sale" in the proven videos | Discounts run 27–57%; it is October | New text before any relaunch |
| Cheap models | 3,499–4,599 items are at or below break-even (~4,600 per single pair) | Never feature them |
