description: Owner's standing rule for every ad campaign on every platform — objective must match the result the business pays for, and everything is checked three times before launch or before saying "done"

  type: feedback
---

1. **Understand the business, then pick the objective from the result they want to pay
   for.** Sales → Sales objective optimising for purchases; leads → Leads; WhatsApp chats →
   a messaging objective optimising for conversations; visits → landing page views. Never
   a sales or leads campaign optimising for clicks.
2. **Three assessments before anything goes live, and before reporting anything as set
   up or done:** the plan against the brief, the created campaign read back from the
   platform against the brief, and a final read-back before activation. Check every
   option, section and setting.

**Why:** on 2026-10-08 a Muzaree Sales campaign was created optimising for link clicks
while the plan, the approval summary and creation all said Sales; the owner caught it in
Ads Manager. He wants this as a rule for every platform and for every AdsPilot user.

**How to apply:** follow AdsPilot's `campaign-setup` skill; run `verify_campaign` after any
create or change; never call a campaign correct without reading it back. Applies to Meta,
TikTok, Google and every other platform.
