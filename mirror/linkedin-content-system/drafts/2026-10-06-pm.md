A spend limit with a default isn't a limit.

When I built the spending cap into what I'm building, the obvious move was a sensible default. Say PKR 5,000 a day, so it works out of the box.

I didn't do it.

Any default is a number the software chose on your behalf. If you never set it, you never decided it. And a ceiling nobody chose isn't a ceiling.

So it fails closed. With no daily and monthly limit set, nothing that can spend money will run. The refusal tells you exactly what to set and where.

Two more things I learned building it:

1. Check the limit against the whole account, not one campaign. Ten campaigns at 5,000 a day is 50,000 a day, even if each one looks small on its own.

2. Use the campaign's real length. My first version assumed every campaign runs for 30 days. A 7-day campaign at 10,000 a day is 70,000 in total, not 300,000. An open-ended one really can reach 300,000 a month, so it's treated that way.

The same goes for any automation that touches money: no silent defaults, check the total, and use real dates.
