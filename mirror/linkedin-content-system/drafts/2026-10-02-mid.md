For the past few weeks I've been building something next to running 1920 Agency.

Here's the short version, and why.

Plenty of businesses already pay for Claude or ChatGPT. It can write a decent ad. It can draft a month of posts.

What it can't do is the actual work: publish to every platform, build the campaign in Meta Ads Manager, check the pixel is tracking, read the results, and do all of it the way an experienced media buyer would.

And the part I care about most: if you connect an AI to an ad account, what stops it spending money nobody approved?

So I'm building the piece in the middle.

Your own AI connects to it. The tool brings the platform knowledge and the tools to do the work. You stay the person who says yes before anything is published or spent.

On Wednesday it launched its first real campaign on our agency's own Meta account. PKR 500 a day. Small on purpose.

I'll write up what I learn here as I go: what works, what Meta refused, and what I got wrong.
