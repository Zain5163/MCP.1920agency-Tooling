A lot of businesses in Pakistan skip their Google Business Profile.

In the UK and Europe, it's often the first place a local customer looks.

That gap matters if you sell across both, or if you run marketing for clients abroad from here.

For a local business, the profile is what turns up for "near me", on Maps and in the panel beside the search results. Before your website, before your ads.

What I'd check this week, if you have one:

1. Is it claimed and verified, and is the category right? The primary category does a lot of the work.
2. Are the hours real, including holidays?
3. Are you posting to it? Updates, offers and events can all go on the profile, and most businesses leave that empty.
4. Are reviews being answered, the bad ones especially?
5. Do the photos show the actual place and people, not stock images?

It's one of the cheapest channels there is, and one of the most ignored.

It's also why Google Business Profile is part of the first stage of what I'm building, alongside the usual social platforms, not an afterthought.
