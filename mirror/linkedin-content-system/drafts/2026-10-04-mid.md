People ask why an agency owner would build a software product instead of just taking on more clients.

Honest answer: I kept watching good marketing work get slowed down by the parts that aren't marketing.

[OWNER: one real example from 1920 Agency in a sentence or two. No client names.]

Posting the same thing to five platforms by hand. Rebuilding a campaign structure we've built a hundred times. Checking whether tracking is actually on. Reporting.

None of that needs a creative mind. It needs someone careful.

AI can do much of it now, but it guesses. And I don't want a guess near a client's budget.

What I wanted was simple to say: a client tells their own AI "run my lead generation ads", and it does it the way a senior media buyer would, then waits for a person to approve before a rupee is spent.

That's what I'm building. Social posting first, then Meta ads, then Google.

It's being built on our own accounts before anyone else's. If it isn't good enough for 1920 Agency, it isn't good enough to sell.
