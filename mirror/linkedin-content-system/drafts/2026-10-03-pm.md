## Post text

One Meta ad can carry a lot more than most people put in it.

These are the limits I build to, checked against Meta's own documentation:

→ 5 primary texts
→ 5 headlines
→ 5 descriptions
→ 10 images
→ 10 videos
→ 5 calls to action

Meta mixes them and learns which combinations work for which people. That's delivery optimisation, not a strict A/B test, but it's a cheap way to give the algorithm options.

The other half is shape. With placement asset customisation, one ad can serve a different file per placement: 4:5 or 1:1 in Feed, 9:16 in Stories and Reels, 1.91:1 for landscape.

Two catches I hit for real are in the slides.

Save this for your next campaign build.

#MetaAds

## Slides

1. **What one Meta ad can actually carry.** The limits, and two catches.
2. **Text:** up to 5 primary texts, 5 headlines, 5 descriptions. Meta rotates them and learns.
3. **Media:** up to 10 images and 10 videos, plus 5 calls to action.
4. **Shapes, one ad:** Feed 4:5 or 1:1 · Stories and Reels 9:16 · Landscape 1.91:1. Placement asset customisation serves each placement its own file.
5. **Catch 1:** multiple texts and placement-specific files can't share one creative. Meta refuses it ("Multiple bodies assets cannot be applied to rule no. 1"). Split it: one ad per text, each keeping every shape.
6. **Catch 2:** I supplied a 1:1 next to a 4:5. It uploaded fine and was never served. I only found out by reading the creative back from Meta. Always read back what you built.
