On my first live Meta campaign, "Website events" was unticked.

I found it by looking in Ads Manager, not from any error. Nothing failed. The ad was running fine.

Here's why it happens, because it can happen to anyone.

If an ad is created without tracking settings, Meta fills in its own defaults: link clicks, landing page views, engagement. The pixel isn't part of that.

On a conversion campaign you'd notice, because the campaign needs the pixel to optimise. On a traffic campaign nothing forces it on. So the ad runs, the clicks come in, and you can't see what those people did on your site afterwards.

What I changed:
→ every ad now carries the account's pixel, whatever the objective
→ on the live ad, the pixel was added alongside Meta's existing tracking, not replacing it, and read back from the API to confirm

The habit I'd suggest:

Before you call a campaign launched, open the ad, scroll to Tracking, and check that Website events is ticked with the right pixel. It takes ten seconds.

Then check that the pixel has fired recently. A pixel that's connected but silent is the same as no pixel.
