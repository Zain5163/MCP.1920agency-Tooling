Your Meta ad can be ACTIVE and not running.

Meta tracks two different statuses, and most people only look at one.

"Status" is the switch you set: on or off.

"Effective status" is what is actually happening: in review, active, disapproved, paused because the campaign above it is paused, and so on.

So an ad can be switched on and disapproved at the same time. It looks fine in a quick glance. It delivers nothing.

Three things I now do on every launch:

1. Read effective status, never just status. In Ads Manager that's the Delivery column, not the toggle.
2. Expect "in review" right after creating an ad. An ad can pass creation and still be rejected hours later. I watched mine go from IN_PROCESS to ACTIVE the same day, but that isn't guaranteed.
3. Never switch a campaign on while one of its ads is rejected. Fix it or remove it first, or you start spending on a campaign that can't deliver what you planned.

In what I'm building, the rejection reason is kept and shown to you, so a disapproved ad shows up as a message with a reason, not as a campaign that has gone quiet.
