I switched off every one of Meta's AI creative enhancements in what I'm building. On purpose.

Meta can now touch up your images, generate text variations, add music and more, automatically.

For some advertisers that's useful. For an agency it's a problem.

A client approves the creative we show them. If Meta then rewrites the copy or changes the image, the ad running under their name isn't the one they approved. And they usually find out from a customer, not from us.

So in what I'm building, enhancements are off by default and you have to opt in deliberately.

Doing that turned out to be fiddly:
→ the old single "standard enhancements" switch is deprecated, and Meta refuses it
→ enhancements now have to be named one by one
→ when I read the ad back from Meta, about 85 of them reported opted out

Two points for anyone running Meta ads:

1. Check what's switched on in your ads. In Ads Manager it's in the creative section of the ad, and some options can be on by default.
2. If you turn some on, tell the client. "Meta may vary the wording" is a much easier conversation before launch than after.

The AI in my tool writes the copy. A person approves it. Meta doesn't get to change it quietly in between.
