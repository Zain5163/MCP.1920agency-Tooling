My first live campaign through the tool I'm building spends PKR 500 a day.

That's deliberate.

It runs in Pakistan for a week, ends on Wednesday, and will spend about PKR 3,500 in total. One ad, five text variants, a real 1920 Agency graphic, sending people to our website.

I wasn't testing the ad. I was testing the whole path, for real:

→ an AI plans the campaign from a plain request
→ the tool checks it against Meta's rules before anything is created
→ everything is created paused
→ I see the full cost and say yes
→ only then does it go live
→ and it's read back from Meta afterwards, not trusted from our own output

It already paid for itself as a test. Meta refused the first attempt on a rule the sandbox never showed (I wrote about it on Friday), and I found a tracking gap in Ads Manager on the live ad.

Neither would have shown up in testing. Both are fixed now.

One more honest note: normally I'd refuse a daily budget this small. Below a certain spend a campaign can't learn much. For an end-to-end test, small was the point.

Results once it ends.
