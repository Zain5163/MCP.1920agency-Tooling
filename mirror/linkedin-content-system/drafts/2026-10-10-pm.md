Meta lead forms fill themselves in. For a B2B offer, that's the problem.

When someone taps a lead ad, the form opens with their details already filled in from their profile. One more tap and you have a lead.

But the email on most people's profile is the one they signed up with years ago. Personal, sometimes old. So the follow-up for your B2B offer lands in an inbox they don't check for work, and the lead goes cold before anyone has spoken to them.

Three settings I'd change on any B2B lead form:

1. Form type: Higher intent, not More volume. It adds a review screen, so people see their answers and confirm before they submit. You get fewer leads, and fewer accidental ones.

2. Ask for work email, not email. In the form it sits under Work information. Then, in the ad's lead quality settings, tick "Require work email", which stops the personal address being filled in for them. It doesn't block Gmail or Yahoo, so still check the domain before a lead reaches sales.

3. Three custom questions at most, and make them multiple choice. Company size, role, when they want to start. Each answer tells sales who to call first. A typed answer on a phone is a reason to close the form.

Then judge the campaign by how many leads sales actually wanted to call, not by cost per lead. A cheap lead nobody can reach is the expensive one.
