# LinkedIn newsletter: "Before It Has a Name"

**Set up 2026-10-05.** A weekly build log on the owner's personal LinkedIn, so
people who like the posts subscribe and never miss what comes next. It stays
mysterious until the product name is final, then the name is revealed here first.

## What to type in LinkedIn's "Create a newsletter" form

| Field | Enter |
|---|---|
| **Newsletter title** | `Before It Has a Name` |
| **How often** | **Weekly** |
| **Description** | `I'm building something that lets the AI you already use run your marketing and operations faster and cheaper, with your approval on every move. For CEOs and marketers. It has no name yet.` |
| **Audience** | CEOs and founders (time, money, faster operations) and marketers (the work itself). Owner's direction, 2026-10-05 |
| **Image** | `logo-300.png` in this folder (300x300, as LinkedIn recommends; `logo-1080.png` is the sharp original) |

Other titles considered: "The Unnamed Build", "Signal Before Launch", "What's
Coming". "Before It Has a Name" won because it *is* the mystery, and it pays off
on reveal day ("…and now it has one"). The title can be edited later.

Other descriptions offered 2026-10-05: "Your AI, doing the work of a marketing team: faster operations, less time, lower cost, and nothing goes live without your approval. Built in public for CEOs and marketers. No name yet." and "Save time and money on marketing and operations with the AI you already use, and approve every move. For CEOs and marketers. Follow the build. It has no name yet."

## How it works on LinkedIn (so nothing surprises you)

- Creating the newsletter invites no one yet. **Your connections and followers are
  invited when you publish the first edition.** So edition 1 is the real launch.
- Editions are LinkedIn **articles**, written in LinkedIn's editor. Our publisher
  cannot post articles (LinkedIn's API does not offer it for personal profiles), so
  each edition is pasted and published by hand. Drafts are prepared here.
- LinkedIn does not give you subscribers' emails. The newsletter builds an audience
  on LinkedIn; a waitlist you own (emails) comes later, at the reveal (STRATEGY §7
  stage 4).
- The newsletter appears in your Featured section after the first edition.

## Rhythm

- **One edition a week**, Thursday 12:00 PKT (morning in the UK and Gulf; the 15:30 slot already holds a post).
- Each edition: what was built, one thing that broke or surprised, one practical
  lesson for marketers, and a hint of what's next. 500–900 words. Real facts only,
  from `AI-Automation\Social-Publisher\PROJECT-LOG.md`; no product name, no client
  names, no invented numbers.
- `editions\` holds the drafts: `edition-NN.md`.

## The subscribe line in posts (starts after edition 1 is published)

Every new post ends with one short line. Rotate these; never two the same in a row:

1. `I write the full story every week in my newsletter, Before It Has a Name. It's on my profile.`
2. `The longer version, with what broke, goes out weekly in Before It Has a Name (on my profile).`
3. `If you want to see what this turns into before it has a name, subscribe to my newsletter on my profile.`
4. `Everything I'm learning building this goes into my weekly newsletter. Link is on my profile.`
5. `The name gets revealed in my newsletter first. Subscribe on my profile so you don't miss it.`

Rules: no link in the post body (subscribe happens from the profile), no "comment
to get" giveaways, one line only, after the post's own ending.
Posts already scheduled before the first edition keep their text; the line is used
from the next drafts on.
